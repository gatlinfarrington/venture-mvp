Creators who sell on Gumroad and live in Google Sheets usually pick one of four paths: CSV export, a Zapier/Make append Zap, a connector add-on, or an AI assistant that pulls data when you ask in plain English. [SheetXAI](https://www.sheetxai.com/) publishes Gumroad-specific guides for that last path. RevenueSheet is aimed at the "always-on ledger + MRR tab" job instead.

This is a fair comparison of **jobs**, not a feature matrix with guessed prices. Every SheetXAI claim below links a public page and a checked date. Claims we could not verify on a public page are omitted. RevenueSheet is pending Google Workspace Marketplace review and is **not** installable today — [join the launch list](/#launch).

## What SheetXAI says it does (public)

SheetXAI describes itself as an AI assistant for Excel and Google Sheets: natural-language commands, data extraction, and connections to business apps ([sheetxai.com](https://www.sheetxai.com/); checked Oct 13, 2026). It is listed on the [Google Workspace Marketplace](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/sheetxai_ai_assistant_for_google_sheets/363574928770) (checked Oct 13, 2026).

On Gumroad specifically, SheetXAI's integration guide says you can ask the sidebar to pull sales into named columns, verify license keys in bulk, and export product catalogs without a CSV round-trip ([How to Connect Gumroad to Google Sheets](https://www.sheetxai.com/integrations/google-sheets/gumroad); checked Oct 13, 2026). The same page states a **7-day free trial** and that the Gumroad integration is included in every SheetXAI plan (checked Oct 13, 2026). We are **not** quoting dollar prices here — we did not find a stable public pricing table to cite on Oct 13, 2026.

## What that job is good at

- Ad-hoc pulls ("sales from the last 90 days into tab Sales")
- One-off analysis and cleanup inside the sheet
- License-key audits driven by a prompt
- Operators who want an AI generalist, not only a revenue pipeline

If your weekly ritual is "ask for a fresh slice of Gumroad data," that shape fits.

## Where a dedicated revenue sync differs

RevenueSheet's planned job at launch is narrower:

- Walk Gumroad (and Lemon Squeezy / Polar / Stripe) history APIs into **stable named tabs**
- Keep refunds and subscriptions in a ledger shape you can QUERY every morning
- Build an **MRR dashboard** from those rows (intervals normalized; statuses hedged the same way every sync)
- Free: one source, manual refresh, full history backfill at launch; Pro (once paid plans open): scheduled refresh across sources (one store per source)

That is automation you configure once, not a prompt you re-type when the monthly update is due. It is also **not** a general AI formula assistant — if you need "write me a VLOOKUP" or "extract this PDF," SheetXAI's published positioning covers that lane and RevenueSheet does not claim it.

For a DIY Gumroad history path without either product, see [Gumroad sales spreadsheet](/blog/gumroad-sales-spreadsheet) (CSV + Apps Script, citing Gumroad's sales API).
