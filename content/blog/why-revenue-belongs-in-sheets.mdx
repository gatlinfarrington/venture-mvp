Most storefront dashboards answer “what sold today.” They do not answer “what does my runway model say if I combine all four sources?”

At launch, RevenueSheet will write orders, subscriptions, and refunds into Google Sheets you own, then build an MRR dashboard tab from those rows. Free covers one source with manual refresh at launch; once paid plans open, Pro schedules sync across Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Gumroad and Stripe (one store per source). RevenueSheet is pending Google Workspace Marketplace review.

If you already live in Sheets for tax and investor updates, the importer is the missing piece (not another hosted chart product). Setup guides live in [Docs](/docs).
