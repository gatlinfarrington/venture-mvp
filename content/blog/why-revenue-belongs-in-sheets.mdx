Most storefront dashboards answer “what sold today.” They do not answer “what does my runway model say if I combine all four stores?”

RevenueSheet writes orders, subscriptions, and refunds into Google Sheets you own, then builds an MRR dashboard tab from those rows. Free covers one source with manual refresh; Pro schedules sync across Lemon Squeezy, Polar, and Gumroad.

If you already live in Sheets for tax and investor updates, the importer is the missing piece (not another hosted chart product). Setup guides live in [Docs](/docs).
